### Program Background: Overview of the PhD Admissions Counselling Service

> The following describes the complete service framework Party A provides to clients, so that Party B understands where the consulting services under this Agreement fit within the overall program. **Party B's actual scope of service under this Agreement remains governed by Article 2** and is not expanded by this background overview.

**Part 01 · Explore the Right Academic Direction**: building the student's profile snapshot, aligning research direction with future goals, identifying target universities/professors/labs and developing outreach plans, and completing an initial gap analysis.

**Part 02 · Build a Competitive Profile**: strengthening research credibility, structuring the complete application case and gap priorities, building a roughly six-month deadline-driven roadmap (including GRE preparation coordination), and developing the student's written narrative framework.

**Part 03 · Apply with End-to-End Support**: personal statement and program-specific essays, recommendation strategy, scholarship assistance and financial planning, application material review and submission, interview preparation, and pre-departure orientation.

**Part 04 · Plan the Investment and Next Steps**: confirming the Fall 2027 application scope and target country/region (determined by academic fit, funding, research opportunity, and affordability), and the counselling fee and excluded costs.

**Party B's consulting services under this Agreement correspond to the "research direction alignment" portion of Part 01 and the "strengthen research credibility" portion of Part 02 above (see Article 2 for details), and do not include essay writing, recommendation letters, scholarship planning, application submission, interview coaching, pre-departure orientation, or other components; those components are performed by Party A itself or through other suppliers.**

### Article 1 · Definitions

1.1 **"Client"** means the student and/or their parent who has signed a PhD admissions counselling service agreement with Party A and receives the services.

1.2 **"Client Contract"** means the paid service agreement Party A enters into with a Client in its own name.

1.3 **"Consulting Services"** means the Research Mentor-related work Party B delivers to Party A under Article 2 of this Agreement, forming part of the services under the Client Contract.

1.4 **"Consultant Fee"** means the fee Party A pays to Party B under Article 5 of this Agreement, as consideration for Party B's Consulting Services.

1.5 **"Circumvention"** means Party B, without Party A's prior written consent, directly or indirectly contacting, negotiating, or reaching any arrangement with a Client regarding commercial matters (including but not limited to pricing, payment, renewal, or providing additional services).

---

### Article 2 · Scope of Services

Under the program framework, Party B provides Consulting Services with respect to the following "Research Mentor" work for Client cases assigned by Party A in writing (including by email); the number of cases and timelines are as assigned by Party A:

**2.1 Research Position & Alignment**

- (a) Helping identify the connection between the Client student's research interests, existing expertise, and future academic/professional goals, to help determine a research direction;
- (b) Providing professional input on the fit between the research direction and target universities, professors, and labs, supporting Party A in finalizing a targeted list of universities/professors and an outreach plan.

**2.2 Strengthen Research Credibility**

- (a) Guiding the Client student to advance and refine their research work, and where necessary, helping develop research that can be published, to strengthen the competitiveness of the application;
- (b) Providing professional input on how the Client student's research materials should be presented in application materials (personal statement, supplementary research statements, etc.);
- (c) Assisting Party A in evaluating the conclusions and priorities of the research-related gap analysis.

**2.3 Other**: The Parties may confirm in writing other service content in which Party B participates (e.g. simulating research-related interview questions), to be attached as an appendix to this Agreement.

2.4 Party B shall provide the Consulting Services personally, and shall not subcontract or delegate the services to any third party without Party A's prior written consent.

---

### Article 3 · Rights and Obligations

**3.1 Party A's Obligations:**

- (a) Assign Client cases to Party B in a timely and clear manner, specifying client background, service standards, and timelines;
- (b) Pay the Consultant Fee to Party B in a timely manner under Article 5;
- (c) Provide Party B with necessary coordination support to perform the Consulting Services (e.g. client communication channels, coordination of materials).

**3.2 Party B's Obligations:**

- (a) Deliver Consulting Services in accordance with the cases assigned by Party A and the agreed standards and timelines, ensuring service quality meets the standards agreed between Party A and the Client;
- (b) Maintain strict confidentiality with respect to Client personal information, academic/research materials, and Party A's business information obtained in the course of providing Consulting Services (see Article 4);
- (c) Not engage in Circumvention as defined in Article 4;
- (d) If unable to deliver services on schedule for any reason, promptly (no later than **[3] business days**) notify Party A in writing and cooperate with Party A in arranging a remedy or substitute consultant.

---

### Article 4 · Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention

4.1 Party B shall maintain strict confidentiality with respect to **Client personal information** (name, contact details, family circumstances, academic/research materials, etc.) and **Party A's business information** (client lists, commercial terms, service methodology, internal tools/templates, etc.) obtained in the course of performing this Agreement, and may use such information solely to deliver the Consulting Services under this Agreement, for no other purpose.

4.2 Party B shall **not**:

- (a) Disclose the foregoing information to any third party other than Party A and Party A's necessary internal personnel;
- (b) Directly or indirectly contact Party A's Clients to engage in, negotiate, or reach any arrangement regarding services, pricing, payment, renewal, or other commercial matters, whether within or outside the scope of this Agreement (i.e. "Circumvention");
- (c) Use client relationships obtained through this Agreement to solicit Clients toward Party B's own or a third party's competing services.

4.3 If Party B breaches this Article, in addition to liability under Article 9, Party A may immediately terminate this Agreement and require Party B to compensate Party A in full for any resulting losses (including to its client relationships and goodwill); Party B shall have no claim to any interest in Party A's subsequent cooperation with or revenue from the relevant Client.

4.4 The confidentiality and non-circumvention obligations under this Article survive termination of this Agreement for **[3] years**.

5.7 Party B's service standards and cooperation requirements during any extended period remain governed by Article 3; if Party B is unable to continue with an extended case for any reason, it shall give Party A **[15]** days' prior written notice so that Party A may arrange a handover or substitute consultant.

### Article 6 · Independent Contractor Status

6.1 The Parties confirm that Party B is an **Independent Contractor**, not an employee, partner, agent, or branch of Party A; this Agreement does not create an employment relationship, and Party B is not entitled to any employee benefits of Party A (except where mandated by applicable law).

6.2 Party B is solely responsible for taxes, insurance, and other statutory obligations arising from the provision of the Consulting Services; Party B has no authority to conduct business, make commitments, or execute documents on Party A's behalf, except as expressly authorized under this Agreement.

6.3 Except for the service delivery, confidentiality, and non-circumvention matters expressly provided for in this Agreement, this Agreement does not create any other legal or business relationship between the Parties.

---

### Article 7 · Intellectual Property

7.1 Any work product Party B creates or develops for a Client or Party A in the course of performing this Agreement (including but not limited to research direction recommendation reports, research guidance notes, and written feedback, but excluding original research output created by the Client or a third party) shall belong to **Party A**; Party B hereby assigns (as an assignment / work made for hire, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law) all intellectual property rights in such work product, while retaining the right to use such work product as necessary to perform the services under this Agreement.

7.2 Intellectual property in the Client student's own original research output, personal experiences, and other information belongs to the student or the original rights holder; Party B may use such materials solely for the purposes of this Agreement.

7.3 Party B's own methodology, general knowledge, and any intellectual property Party B held prior to signing this Agreement remain Party B's property; Party A obtains only a license to use such items for the purpose of completing the Client services.

---

### Article 8 · Client Disputes

8.1 Client complaints, claims, litigation, or arbitration arising from the content, quality, or effectiveness of the Consulting Services shall be **handled by, and are the responsibility of, Party A** (Party A bears full performance liability to the Client); however, for Client disputes arising from **Party B's own fault** (e.g. Party B's breach of the agreed service standards, engaging in Circumvention, or other fault) and any resulting losses to Party A, Party A shall have the right to seek reimbursement from Party B.

8.2 If Party B receives any complaint, claim, or legal document from a Client relating to the scope of services under this Agreement, Party B shall forward it to Party A within **[3]** business days of receipt, cooperate with Party A in handling the matter, and shall not respond on its own or make any commitments.

---

### Article 9 · Liability for Breach

9.1 If either Party breaches this Agreement, the Parties shall seek to resolve the matter amicably through discussion; if no resolution is reached and the breach causes actual loss to the other Party, the breaching Party shall provide compensation.

9.2 Neither Party shall be liable for breach where its failure to perform this Agreement results from force majeure, provided that it promptly notifies the other Party and takes reasonable measures to mitigate the loss.

9.3 Except for liability arising under Article 4 (Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention), either Party's liability to the other under this Agreement is capped at the total Consultant Fees actually received by Party B under this Agreement in the preceding **[6]** months (excluding losses caused by intentional misconduct, gross negligence, or breach of Article 4).

---

### Article 10 · Term and Termination

10.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature by both Parties and remains in effect for **[1] year**; if neither Party raises an objection within **[30]** days before expiry, the Agreement automatically renews for one additional year.

10.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving **[30]** days' prior written notice to the other Party; for any Client case that has been assigned but not yet completed, the Parties shall agree on a proper handover or complete the case before termination takes effect.

10.3 If either Party materially breaches this Agreement (including a material breach of Article 4) and fails to cure the breach within **[15]** days of written notice, the non-breaching Party may terminate this Agreement immediately.

10.4 **Effect of Termination**: Any Consultant Fee unpaid as of termination shall still be paid by Party A in accordance with Article 5; Article 4 (Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention), Article 7 (Intellectual Property), Article 8 (Client Disputes, limited to service matters arising during the term), and Article 9 (Liability for Breach) survive termination of this Agreement.

---

### Article 11 · Miscellaneous

11.1 **Notices**: All notices shall be given in writing (including by email) to the contact details set out at the head of this Agreement, and shall be deemed delivered upon such notice.

11.2 **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement (together with any case assignments, appendices, etc. confirmed in writing by the Parties) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding this cooperation, superseding all prior oral or written understandings.

11.3 **Amendment**: Any amendment to this Agreement is effective only if made in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.4 **Severability**: If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11.5 **Non-Exclusivity**: Except as otherwise agreed in writing, this Agreement does not constitute an exclusive arrangement restricting Party B to working only with Party A; however, Party B's services with respect to Client cases shall comply with Article 4.

11.6 **Language**: This Agreement is prepared in both Chinese and English; in the event of any conflict between the two versions, the **Chinese version** shall prevail (unless the Parties otherwise agree in writing that the English version shall prevail).
